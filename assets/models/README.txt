Put your own 3D models in this folder.

Recommended format: .glb (glTF binary).
Recommended tools: Blender, Blockbench, MagicaVoxel.

Read docs/3D_MODELS.md for the scale rules (1 unit = 1 metre, origin at the
bottom, -Z is forward) and how to add a model to a scene.

Suggested folders:
  assets/models/props/      rocks, barrels, crates, fences
  assets/models/nature/     trees, mushrooms, crystals
  assets/models/weapons/    pickaxe, swords
  assets/models/characters/ the player and the bosses
